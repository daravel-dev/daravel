## 1.13.0

- First release: spatie/laravel-activitylog v4 on its `activity_log` table (`CreateActivityLogTable`), shared with Laravel. `LogsActivity` and `ActivityLogObserver` (`created`, `updated` with old values, `deleted`, `restored`), Spatie's `LogOptions`, `activity()` with placeholders, causers from the request's user or `ActivityLog.causedBy`, `LogBatch`, `ActivityLog.withoutLogs`, `Activity` with Spatie's scopes, `subject()` and `causer()`, `CausesActivity`, the `activitylog:clean` command (`package:daravel_activitylog/commands.dart`), the `ACTIVITY_LOGGER_*` settings.
