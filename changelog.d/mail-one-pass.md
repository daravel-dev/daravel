### Fixed

- Security: markdown mail and mail addresses are read in one pass. The plain-text version of a markdown mail (`markdownToText`, made for every markdown `Mailable` without its own text), the `<x-mail::button>` and `<x-mail::panel>` components of `MailTheme.render`, and `MailAddress.parse` (`Mail.to('Name <address>')` and the other recipient strings) used regular expressions that scanned to the end of the text from every opening tag, bracket or address without its closing one: a 64 KiB body took up to 80 s and a 64 KiB address 57 s, in the queue worker or in the request that sent the mail. An application that put text from its users into a mail (a contact form, a comment notification) was affected. The results are the same as before. Since 1.9.0. Until you upgrade, limit the length of user text that goes into mail (`max:` rules) and validate addresses with the `email` rule.
