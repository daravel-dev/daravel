### Fixed

- Security: `gt`, `gte`, `lt`, `lte`, `decimal` and `multiple_of` read a numeric value in time linear in its length. They turned its digits into one big integer (`BigInt.parse`), which takes time quadratic in their number: a field of 256 000 digits held a request for about a second, 1 000 000 digits for 35 to 46 s, and the default body limit (50 MiB) allows far more; any endpoint validating such a field with one of these rules was reachable. The value is now kept as its digits and compared digit by digit (`multiple_of` takes the digits modulo the rule's factor), with the same results as before on every input and as Laravel 13's exact `BigDecimal` comparison. Since 1.22.0. Until you upgrade, lower the body limit (`HTTP_MAX_BODY`, or at the proxy in front of the application) to what your endpoints need: the cost grows with the square of the digits a field can carry.
