### Added

- Coordinator bulk credit (Blueprint #141, K1b): a worker's `RemoteCoordinatorChannel` keeps at most `bulkWindow` (16) bulk calls unanswered (`sendBulk`, or `callBulk` on any `CoordinatorChannel`), so the coordinator's port holds at most workers × 16 of them whatever the load, and critical calls (rate limits, locks) no longer wait behind a flood of broadcasts. Bulk calls beyond the window wait in order in a buffer of `bulkBuffer` (1 024) and go together as one `CoordinatorBatch` message once half the window is free; their answers come back together (`CoordinatorBatchReply`) when ready at once. A call gives its credit back with its answer or at its own timeout. A full buffer refuses a broadcast (`CoordinatorOverloaded`, dropped and told as `BroadcastShed`) and drops the oldest metrics, worker report or Horizon record (its future fails with `CoordinatorOverloaded`). A worker's last bulk calls are flushed (at most 2 s) when its application shuts down. `Coordinator.stats()` gains `port_messages` (a batch is one).

### Changed

- The coordinator learns its workers from one `CoordinatorHello` each channel sends when it opens, instead of from every request, and works out a call's priority only when it is not healthy (`served_by_priority` is sampled one call in 16 while healthy): the healthy path no longer pays for K1's bookkeeping on every message.
- The coordinator is `lagging` or `saturated` only on evidence of a queue: a probe's wait counts as lag only when at least `Coordinator.queueEvidence` (16) other messages were handled meanwhile. A wait with fewer (a busy host, a GC pause) or a late timer is starvation, shown as `starved_ms`/`starved_peak_ms` in `stats()` (`coordinator_starved_seconds`/`coordinator_starved_peak_seconds` in the metrics) and never a reason to refuse work. Before, a merely busy host made the coordinator refuse every bulk call.
- Rate limits are always asked of the coordinator; they no longer fail open at once while it says it is saturated, which turned limits off on a busy host (K1: `supervisor_test` failed 19 of 27 times under CPU load, main 0 of 30). Only their own timeout applies the failure policy, as before K1.
- Priorities per operation: a broadcast `publish`, a metrics `publish`, a worker report `publish` and a Horizon `record` are bulk (`PrioritizedService.bulkOperations`); subscriptions, the metrics scrape (`collect`, critical) and reading reports are not, so `/metrics` and new subscriptions keep working while the coordinator is saturated. Announcements and the workers' short-circuit name `service.operation`.
- A coordinator refusal is recognised by `CoordinatorReply.errorKind` (`CoordinatorErrorKind`), not by its text. A saturation recovers only after `recoverySamples` measurements below it and not while calls put off are waiting, and new calls go behind those calls, so one worker's calls keep their order. A broadcast rethrown in a retryable job keeps its original stack trace. A `RemoteCoordinatorChannel`'s timeout sweep runs detached (`runDetached`), not in the zone of the request whose call started it.

### Upgrade notes

- New public names `CoordinatorBatch`, `CoordinatorBatchReply`, `CoordinatorHello`, `CoordinatorErrorKind`, `BulkOverflow` and `BulkCoordinatorCalls` in `package:daravel` (an application class of one of these names and the barrel import give an ambiguous-import error; `hide` it or rename yours). Code that sends raw `CoordinatorRequest`s to a coordinator's port without a `RemoteCoordinatorChannel` is not told of its health unless it sends a `CoordinatorHello` first.
