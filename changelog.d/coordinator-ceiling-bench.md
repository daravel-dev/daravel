### Added

- Coordinator benchmarks (Blueprint #141, K0): `bench/bin/coordinator_ceiling.dart` measures one coordinator driven by 1, 4, 8 and 16 client isolates (rate limit attempts, locks, `process` cache reads, broadcast publishes, an empty service and one-way messages), a rate limit attempt alone and under a publish flood, and the accuracy of rate limit leases; `bench/bin/coordinator_census.dart` counts the coordinator messages per request of the bench scenarios from `/metrics`. `bench.dart` gains scenario 14 (a bearer token, a rate limit and a cached read), mirrored in the Laravel overlay with Sanctum. `bench/laravel/compare.sh` passes Laravel's settings as environment variables (a fresh clone has no `.env` in the overlay, so Octane stopped on boot with `MissingAppKeyException`), stops with the server's log when Laravel never answers `/up`, and takes `CACHE_VARIANT` (`process` or `redis`), `SANCTUM_LAST_USED_AT` and `PG_POOL`. Results of 2026-11-10 in [bench/README.md](bench/README.md#the-coordinator-ceiling-census-and-saturation-blueprint-141-k0).
