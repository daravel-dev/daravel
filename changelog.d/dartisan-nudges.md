### Added

- `dartisan` closing lines (Blueprint #137, C3). After a successful `build`, `upgrade`, `self-update`, `test`, or the first `migrate` of a project (the one that creates its `migrations` table), one dim line thanks the sponsors, with the one link, followed by `(hide this with: dartisan sponsor --hide)`; it is never shown after a failure, in CI (`CI`, `GITHUB_ACTIONS`, `GITLAB_CI`, `TF_BUILD`), off a terminal, with `DARTISAN_NO_NUDGE=1` or after `dartisan sponsor --hide`, and at most once in 24 hours per machine (`~/.dartisan/last_nudge`). The line changes daily among three; it thanks or informs and never asks. `dartisan new` ends its next steps with a fixed line, under the same conditions but without the 24 hours. `Nudges`, `nudgeLines`, `nudgeFor`, `nudgeCommands` and `ciVariables` are public; `DartisanRunner(nudges:)` and `GlobalDartisanRunner(nudges:)` replace them in tests, and a project command may call `DartisanConsole.celebrate()`. `security:audit` colours through `Console` (`--no-color`, `NO_COLOR` and a pipe turn it off; the uncoloured and coloured text are byte for byte as before), and `Console.box` ends a line it cuts with `…`.

### Upgrade notes

- `dartisan` (C3): new public names `Nudges`, `nudgeLines`, `nudgeFor`, `nudgeCommands`, `ciVariables` and `DartisanConsole` in `package:dartisan_cli`; a project command file that declares one of them and imports the barrel gets an ambiguous-import error (`hide` them or rename yours). `security:audit` without `--output` no longer colours when stdout is a pipe on a terminal that supports ANSI escapes, and honours `--no-color` and `NO_COLOR`; with `--format=json`, `sarif` or `--output` nothing changes.
