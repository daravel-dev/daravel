### Fixed

- What the server holds for a WebSocket handler that does not read stays within its bound (64 KiB, or the route's `maxPayloadBytes` if smaller, plus one message or 64 KiB of frames) whatever the size of one read from the connection: the server stops parsing there, and the rest of the last read waits unparsed until the handler reads (one read per connection, as large as the system's receive buffer lets it be), while TCP holds the client back.
- However a WebSocket session ends (the client's close or its going away, the server's close, a slow client, a protocol violation, the server stopping), nothing it held stays held once its `done` completed, also while its handler is paused, and its connection is destroyed at the latest after the close grace. A handler that resumes after the server closed gets none of the messages it had not read, and `closeCode` no longer turns into `1006` when the connection ends after this side closed.
- From the moment the server starts closing a WebSocket (the application's `close()`, a slow client, a shutdown), the client breaks the protocol, or the session ended, what the client still sends is counted once, never started again, and read for at most 4 MiB more (about what can be in flight), instead of being read and parsed at the client's rate. Past that, while the server is closing or after a violation, it stops reading: what waits to be sent and the close frame still go out, and the connection ends with the close's grace, at most 11 seconds after the close. Once the session ended the connection is cut (a close frame still waiting for a client that does not read is then dropped). After the session ended nothing is parsed at all. A client that goes away while a session ends is seen at once, also after a protocol violation and after the handler cancelled its subscription during the close, so no timer or connection of the session is left for the grace.
