### Added

- The `/reverb` dashboard (Blueprint #135, R3b): `withReverb(dashboard: '/reverb', middleware: [...])` (in place of R3's `dashboard:` middleware list and `dashboardPath:`, before the package's first release) serves a page from `package:daravel/html.dart` with tabs Overview (connections per application and isolate, channels by type, messages per second, fan-out p50/p95), Limits (slow consumers, rate limited, refusals by reason), Apps (without secrets) and Workers, a CSP nonce per response and every value written as text; `GET /reverb/api/stats` (what `reverb:stats --json` prints), `/api/apps`, `/api/stream` (server-sent events every second, a random `retry:`, ended by the drain) and `POST /api/restart` (`reverb:restart`). Outside `local` and `testing` every route answers 403 until `middleware:` guards it; `dashboard: ''` serves none. The numbers come from `/metrics`: this process's, or a `reverb:start` server's at `REVERB_METRICS_URL`. `ReverbReport` moves to the main library and reads fan-out percentiles, `reverb_rate_limited_total` and `realtime_slow_clients_total`; `ReverbDashboard`, `reverbPage`, `reverbTabs`. `pub:add reverb` registers `.withReverb(middleware: ['auth', 'can:viewReverb'])` and prints the `viewReverb` gate; `daravel_pulse`'s *Reverb* card links to the dashboard (`Pulse.reverbPath`). Guide: [Broadcasting](docs/guide/broadcasting.md#reverb-dashboard).

### Upgrade notes

- `daravel_reverb` (R3b): new public names `ReverbDashboard`, `reverbPage`, `reverbTabs`, and `ReverbReport` now in `package:daravel_reverb/daravel_reverb.dart` (an application class of one of these names and the barrel import give an ambiguous-import error; `hide` it or rename yours). `withReverb(dashboard:)` is the dashboard's path (`/reverb`, `''` for none) and `middleware:` guards it, in place of R3's `dashboard:` middleware list and `dashboardPath:` (neither released). `daravel_pulse`: `/pulse/api/stats` gains `reverb_path` (null without the dashboard).
