### Fixed

- `daravel_reverb_client`: `connect()` while a reconnect was waiting opened a second socket next to the first, so every event arrived twice; a listener of `onConnectionStateChange` that called `connect()` on `unavailable` threw ("Cannot fire new event"), and the waiting reconnect later replaced the connection it opened. One connection at a time now: opening one cancels the waiting reconnect and closes the previous socket, whose late callbacks are ignored, and the states follow in order.
- `daravel_reverb_client`: an event of an encrypted channel that did not open (not base64, a nonce or key of the wrong length, not UTF-8) threw out of the socket's listener; it is dropped and reported on `onError` as `{type: 'DecryptionError', channel, event}`, as pusher-js logs and drops it. A server `activity_timeout` that is zero, negative or not finite is ignored, and a positive one is at least one second (zero pinged without a pause, `1e400` threw).
- `daravel_reverb_client`: a channel left while its subscription was on its way stayed subscribed on the server; it is now left once the server confirms the subscription, as pusher-js does, and joining it again before that keeps it. Errors of the WebSocket reach `onError` as `{type: 'WebSocketError', error}` (pusher-js), instead of being swallowed.
